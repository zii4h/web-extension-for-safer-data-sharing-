JITO does not edit the original file. File contents stay in this tab until you clear it or close it. Downloads remain on your device.

What this version covers

JPEG and static PNG: detects metadata blocks, then exports rendered pixels as a fresh PNG. All source metadata is discarded together. Orientation is baked into the image; color and file size may change.

UTF-8 TXT, MD, CSV, LOG: detects likely email addresses, US SSNs, payment-card numbers with a valid checksum, and some secret-token patterns. Export is plain text.

No PDF, Office, HEIC, animated images, OCR, visible-content analysis, or universal upload interception. Detection can miss sensitive information and produce false positives. A finding is a review cue, not a guarantee.
